Currently in private testing.

Version date · 2026-11-17

Sisyphus Almanac is a daily workspace for schedules, tasks, notes, and related material on the Mac. Version 1.1.0 brings rapid logging and end-of-day review into Today.

- **Daily Log and Daily Review.** Preview several lines of entries before saving. Use keyboard symbols to add tasks, event drafts, notes, and ideas or flag priorities. Move unfinished tasks to a date you choose and keep a history of the move.
- **Calendar and reference material.** Browse day, week, or month views and find related material from an event. Open the source in Apple Notes, Obsidian, or Apple Mail.
- **Mail and Finance.** Browse up to 12 inbox messages per active Apple Mail account and see three on Today. Connect a selected Excel workbook, including one in a local OneDrive folder.
- **Menu bar and Quick Capture.** Use the Sisyphus icon for a quick look at the day, or choose a calendar or reminders list and save a new item.

Requires macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later. Data access follows macOS permissions and the accounts and folders configured on the Mac.
