## Keeping track of the day

Check your schedule and reminders in Today. In Daily Log, draft tasks, events, and notes across several lines and preview them before saving. Daily Review lets you reflect on the day and move unfinished tasks to a date you choose.

Browse your schedule in the weekly calendar and open related messages in Apple Mail or notes in Obsidian. In Finance, you can connect an Excel workbook selected from your OneDrive folder.
