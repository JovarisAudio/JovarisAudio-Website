1. **Explore the samples.** Select **View practice samples** on the dashboard. Expand one of the creator’s four piano and rhythm logs, or open **Weekly Review** for a summary by week. Samples are available without signing in.
2. **Start your own log.** Select **Sign in with ChatGPT** in the hosted app. If you are on the Jovaris Audio sample page, choose **Open personal workspace** to reach the hosted app.
3. **Log one session.** Select **Log practice** or use **Quick Add**. Start with the date, category, duration, and what you practiced. Add BPM, success rate, a blocker, or an output link when useful.
4. **Practice sight singing.** Choose an interval from unison to octave or the combined exercise. Level 1 is play first and sing back. Level 2 is play and sing together. Level 3 is sing first and check the notes on the keyboard.
5. **Plan the next session.** Use **Weekly Review** to see practice time and recurring blockers. Use **Projects** to track arrangements in Logic Projects and mixing sessions by song in Pro Tools.

Personal records are saved to your signed-in account. Use **English** or **한국어** at the top of the page to switch languages.
